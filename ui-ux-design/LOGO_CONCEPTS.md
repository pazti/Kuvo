# Kuvo Logo Concepts

## What the mark needs to do

The mark should be recognizable at app-icon size, work in one color, feel active rather than corporate, and connect messaging with the idea of a living network.

## Concept A — Pulse bubble (recommended)

**Shape:** A compact speech bubble with a three-point rising pulse inside.
**Signal:** Conversation, movement, and the Kuvo network being alive.
**Neo-brutalist treatment:** Green solid fill, black outline, and one offset black shadow.
**Why it leads:** It explains the product category without spelling out a chat bubble and keeps a memorable internal shape.

First-pass asset: [`logo/kuvo-pulse-mark.svg`](logo/kuvo-pulse-mark.svg)

## Concept B — K peak

**Shape:** A blocky letter K whose upper arm forms a peak.
**Signal:** Kuvo ownership and upward momentum.
**Best use:** App icon, favicon, avatar stamp, and compact product chrome.
**Trade-off:** More ownable, but less immediately connected to messaging.

## Concept C — Open link

**Shape:** Two rounded links opening toward one another inside a square frame.
**Signal:** Connection, groups, and people finding each other.
**Best use:** Community or business messaging surfaces.
**Trade-off:** Strong network meaning, but it can resemble a generic chain or link icon.

## Recommendation

Start with Concept A for the consumer launch. Test the mark in black, white, and Kuvo Green at 24px, 48px, and app-icon sizes before finalizing. Keep the wordmark separate so the mark can work independently in navigation and notifications.

## Wordmark direction

- Use a lowercase or title-case `Kuvo` wordmark with a sturdy geometric sans.
- Keep the wordmark black or Ink on light backgrounds.
- Avoid adding a second icon inside the wordmark.
- Let the mark carry the energy; keep the wordmark simple.
