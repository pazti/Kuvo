# Strategy

## Sequencing (Locked)

1. **Campus MVP** — Get real usage and engagement inside universities
2. **Widen to Young Nigeria** — Expand beyond campuses to cities and friend/family networks
3. **Business Messaging Platform** — Launch once we have meaningful reach (built in parallel)

## Why Campus First

- Students already live in dense chat groups
- Easy to seed and grow organically
- Creates authentic network effects
- Avoids the classic chicken-and-egg problem of starting with businesses

## Monetization

- Consumer app: Free
- Future revenue: Business messaging (naira pricing), possible premium features, partnerships
- No user fees at launch

## Positioning

Not “the student app”.  
Campus is only the entry point. Branding and product remain general-audience from day one.
