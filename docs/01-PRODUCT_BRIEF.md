# Kuvo — Product Brief

**Last updated:** September 2026  
**Status:** Internal founding document

## One-liner

Kuvo is a free messaging platform built for young Nigerians. We start on campuses, earn real usage, then expand to the wider youth market and later launch a business messaging platform.

## The Problem

- Nigerian personal and business communication runs almost entirely on WhatsApp.
- From October 2026, Meta begins charging businesses for WhatsApp Business Platform messages (dollar-denominated).
- There is no credible local alternative that understands how Nigerians actually chat (commerce, payments, groups, low bandwidth).
- Existing alternatives (Telegram, Signal) are not built for the Nigerian context.

## Our Approach

We do **not** start by selling to businesses.

We start by winning young users first (especially on campuses), build real engagement and network effects, then offer businesses a naira-priced, reliable messaging platform once we have reach.

## Core Principles

1. Consumer side is free forever.
2. Campus is the beachhead, not the ceiling.
3. One name and brand for both consumer and business surfaces.
4. High readability and performance on low-end Android devices.
5. Local ownership and pricing (naira) when we reach businesses.

## Target Users (Phase 1)

- University and polytechnic students across Nigeria
- Young people aged 16–28
- Heavy group chat and campus community users

## Success Metrics (Early)

- Daily active users on campus
- Group creation and retention
- Organic invite rate
- Time spent in app
