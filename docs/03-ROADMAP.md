# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Campus MVP (Current Focus)

- Core messaging (1:1 and groups)
- Media sharing (optimized for low bandwidth)
- Status / Stories-style updates
- Basic profiles
- Campus discovery / group suggestions
- Clean, fast, reliable experience

## Phase 2 — Widen the Net

- Expand beyond campuses
- Improved discovery and invites
- Performance and reliability hardening
- Push notification excellence

## Phase 3 — Business Surface

- Business messaging platform
- Naira pricing
- Payment integrations (Paystack / Flutterwave)
- Admin tools and analytics

## Parallel Work

Design system, branding, and technical foundation continue across all phases.
