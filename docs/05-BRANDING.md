# Branding

## Name

**Kuvo**  
Meaning: “The pulse” — a network that is always alive and moving.

## Brand Personality

- Energetic
- Trustworthy
- Modern
- Community-oriented
- Confident but not arrogant

## Voice & Tone

Clear, friendly, slightly energetic.  
English first. Light Pidgin can appear in marketing later, never in core product UI.

## Visual Identity

- Clean and contemporary
- Strong but soft color accents
- Avoid heavy neo-brutalism or overly playful game aesthetics
- Designed to work for both campus users and future business features
